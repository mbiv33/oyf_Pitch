AthleteFO

The front office for athletes and families building enterprise value in the NIL era.

Move the athlete household from talent for hire to franchise owner. Structure, data, IP control, and repeatable decision systems, built as a recurring platform.

Why Now

The window opened four years ago. The infrastructure layer still has not been built.

2021 NIL rules take effect

The NCAA allows college athletes to earn from name, image, and likeness. High school state laws begin to follow within 18 months.

2022 to 2024 Youth sports spend accelerates

Household spending on youth sports grew past $40B and continues to climb. The top 25% of families now spend $12K+ per athlete annually.

2024 to 2025 First NIL era enters the pros

The first wave of athletes who grew up commercializing at 15 and 16 is now entering professional leagues. Their younger siblings and teammates watched the whole thing.

Now The tools have not caught up

Agents, collectives, and software still treat the family as a pass-through. No one is selling an operating layer to the household itself.

NIL made every recruitable athlete a commercial entity. The category for managing that reality at the household level does not yet exist.

The Problem

High-investment families are running a business without a business.

Composite household, 2024

The Reyes family has spent roughly $47K over three seasons on travel softball, showcases, and private coaching for their 16-year-old daughter. She is a D1 recruiting target.

A regional apparel brand offers her a $4,500 NIL agreement. The family has 72 hours to review a 14-page contract, decide whether to form an entity, and think through multi-state tax exposure. Their travel coach knows "a guy" who handles it for $1,200 flat.

Six months later, they learn the contract granted the brand perpetual rights to her likeness in any derivative product, including categories she would later be offered by a larger brand at 10x the price.

Structural issue 01

Conflict of interest baked in

Agents, collectives, and management firms are paid on deal volume or roster stability. None are paid to protect the household.

Structural issue 02

Professional problems, hobbyist tools

Entity formation, 1099 tracking, multi-state tax, IP licensing. Real problems handled through group texts and Google Docs.

Structural issue 03

No operating layer exists

Software sits on the brand or agent side. Education sits at the athlete level. Nothing organizes decisions at the household level.
